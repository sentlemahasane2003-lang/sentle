The Mountain Kingdom

Welcome to Lesotho

Lesotho is a high-altitude mountain kingdom completely surrounded by South Africa. Known as the "Roof of Africa," it offers pristine mountain air, majestic rivers, unique Basotho culture, and unforgettable landscapes.

AfriSki Mountain Resort

Located at 3,222 meters in the Maloti Mountains of Butha-Buthe, AfriSki offers thrilling winter snow sports, snowboarding, and year-round high-altitude mountain biking and trail running.
