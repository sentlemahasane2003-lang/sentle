Waterfall

Maletsunyane Falls

Semonkong, Maseru District

A magnificent 192m waterfall where visitors can enjoy breathtaking cliff lookouts, pony trekking, and the world's longest commercial abseil.

From M 750 Book a Tour
Mountain Pass

Sani Pass

Mokhotlong District Border

An iconic zigzagging mountain pass reaching 2,876m elevation, renowned for thrilling 4x4 climbs and the highest pub in Africa.

From M 950 Book a Tour
Highland Dam

Katse Dam

Thaba-Tseka District

The centerpiece of the Lesotho Highlands Water Project, featuring an imposing 185m curved dam wall, alpine botanical garden, and boat cruises.

From M 650 Book a Tour
Cultural Heritage

Thaba Bosiu

Maseru District (24 km East)

A sacred mountain fortress where King Moshoeshoe I united the Basotho. Features a cultural village, historical museum, and royal burial ground.

From M 500 Book a Tour
Winter Sports

AfriSki Mountain Resort

Butha-Buthe District

Southern Africa's premier ski and snowboarding destination with seasonal snow making, ski lifts, high-altitude trail running, and mountain biking.

From M 1,200 Book a Tour
National Park

Tsehlanyane National Park

Leribe District

An untamed mountain wilderness protecting rare indigenous sub-alpine indigenous woodlands, crystal clear mountain streams, and serene nature trails.

From M 800 Book a Tour
